Hi, I'm Jianping (Mike) Ye! I am a Ph.D. candidate in [Applied Mathematics & Statistics, and Scientific Computation (AMSC)](https://amsc.umd.edu/) at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by [Michel Wedel](https://www.rhsmith.umd.edu/directory/michel-wedel). I specialize in applied statistics and expect to graduate in May 2027. My dissertation is *Machine Learning Models for Human Attention Prediction and their Applications*.

My research connects statistical machine learning, computer vision, and consumer behavior. I develop models that predict where people look, how long they attend to visual content, and how these insights can improve advertising and image generation. My work spans theory-informed contextual advertising, eye-tracking and gaze inference, video saliency, and human-preference alignment of generative models.

I have worked as a Quantitative User Experience Research Intern at **Google** (May–August 2026) and a Data Scientist Intern at **IBM** (September 2025–April 2026). Since March 2026, I have also collaborated with **Unravel Neuromarketing Research** on video-ad attention and viewer engagement.

Before UMD, I earned my B.S. in Applied Mathematics at UCLA, with a specialization in Computing and a minor in Bioinformatics. I worked with [Samy Wu Fung](https://swufung.github.io/) on distributed optimization and with [Chenfanfu Jiang](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~cffjiang/) and his group on physics-based simulation.

## Research interests

* **AI for advertising:** quantitative marketing, contextual advertising, and consumer attention.
* **Eye tracking and computer vision:** gaze-time prediction, fixation inference, and dynamic video saliency.
* **Generative AI:** prompt optimization and alignment with human preferences.
* **Statistical learning and optimization:** probabilistic modeling, uncertainty quantification, and distributed algorithms.

## Research highlights

* **[AdGazer](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251396114)**, published in the *Journal of Marketing*, combines theory-informed visual and semantic features to predict attention and improve contextual ad placement.
* At **CVPR 2026**, I presented **[SIGN]({{ '/publication/2025-01-25-SIGN-number-4' | relative_url }})** as an **oral presentation** and **[AdGaze-3500]({{ '/publication/2026-06-01-AdGaze-3500' | relative_url }})** as a **poster** in their respective workshops.
* **[ViASNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29302)**, currently **submitted and under review**, models attention over time in video ads. **[IPGO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21812)** explores efficient prompt-level adaptation of text-to-image models.
