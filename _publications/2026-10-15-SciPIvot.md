SciΠvot evaluates scientific judgment using 248 decisions drawn from 26 research projects across six fields. Each case asks a model to choose between an agent's original plan and a human-corrected alternative using the available project history. The study compares model and human decisions and investigates how reasoning effort, access to context, and reconsideration affect performance.
