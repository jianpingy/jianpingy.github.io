IPGO adapts text-to-image generation by learning additional prompt embeddings while keeping the underlying diffusion model fixed. Low-rank structure, rotations, and constraints on the learned embeddings make the adaptation efficient and help preserve the original prompt's meaning. IPGO+ adds attention between the original and inserted embeddings to support learning across prompts.

The study evaluates alignment with aesthetics, image–text consistency, and human preferences in both individual-prompt and prompt-batch settings.

The current arXiv title reflects the May 2025 revision. The earlier title was *IPGO: Indirect Prompt Gradient Optimization on Text-to-Image Generative Models with High Data Efficiency*.
