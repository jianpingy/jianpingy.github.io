**Status:** Submitted and currently under review. A preprint is available on arXiv.

ViASNet predicts how visual attention changes during video advertisements. Its 3D U-Net-based architecture incorporates visual information, audio, and scene semantics. The study evaluates predictions against eye-tracking observations from 151 video ads and explores how the components affect performance.

Frame-level saliency entropy provides a diagnostic for identifying scenes that may struggle to engage viewers, with an illustration on 15 previously unseen ads.
