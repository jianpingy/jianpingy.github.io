I presented this work as a **poster at CVPR 2026**, in the Workshop on Emerging Directions in Data for Multimodal Foundation Models.

AdGaze-3500 introduces a benchmark of 3,531 advertising images and their editorial contexts, with aggregate gaze times for whole ads and their brand, text, and pictorial regions. The study evaluates 20 large multimodal models in zero-shot and fine-tuned settings.

Although direct zero-shot prediction is challenging, model-generated scanpaths provide useful signals for learning gaze-time predictors. The benchmark supports research on how multimodal models represent human attention to advertising.
