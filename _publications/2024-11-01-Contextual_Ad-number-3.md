AdGazer combines a theoretical account of advertising attention with visual feature extraction, multimodal language models, and XGBoost. It predicts attention to ads and brands and uses these predictions to assign advertisements to suitable media contexts. Evaluation on 3,531 display ads examines predictive accuracy, generalization to new ads, and the benefits of context-aware placement.

Published in the July 2026 issue of the *Journal of Marketing*, volume 90, issue 4, pages 122–144. First published online on November 2, 2025. An earlier manuscript circulated as *Contextual Advertising with Theory-Informed Machine Learning*.
